# Cover letter for *Sensors*

Dear Editors,

We submit “Visibility-Aware UAV Viewpoint Selection for Ground Robot Localization in Simulated Orchards” for consideration as an Article in *Sensors*.

The study examines how canopy occlusion and bearing geometry affect camera-based localization of a ground robot using two markers observed by a UAV. All policies share the same estimator and sensor model. We compare a geometric lookup table, a bounded Gaussian and a uniform prior while generating marker pairs by direct ray casting in separate simulated layouts.

The center and edge scenarios give different results. Near the canopy edge, the lookup table gave 0.749° heading RMSE compared with 2.343° for the Gaussian prior at the same motion weight, with differences in flight distance and detection rates. We also compare against fixed stations tuned over azimuth and distance, which reveal the accuracy cost of shorter adaptive flight. The supplement contains the simulator, plotting scripts and seed-level results.

We confirm that the manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree to its submission to *Sensors*.
