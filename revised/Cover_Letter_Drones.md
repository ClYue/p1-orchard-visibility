# Cover letter for *Drones*

Dear Editors,

We submit “Visibility-Aware UAV Viewpoint Selection for Ground Robot Localization in Simulated Orchards” for consideration as an Article in *Drones*.

The manuscript studies how an aerial observer should move to localize a ground robot when the canopy can block either of its two visual markers. It compares a speed-limited adaptive policy with 48 fixed stations spanning distance and azimuth. We also compare three visibility priors while evaluating all policies against direct ray casting in separately generated orchards.

The results show where viewpoint adaptation adds value and where a simple fixed station remains competitive. Near the canopy edge, the lookup-table policy preserved more localization accuracy than the Gaussian and uniform alternatives at the same motion weight. Compared with the best-heading fixed station at that edge, one tested lookup-table setting flew 15.6% less while increasing heading RMSE from 0.562° to 0.749°. These simulations connect the UAV's viewpoint and travel distance to the ground localization task. The accompanying supplement provides the simulator, plotting scripts and seed-level results.

We confirm that the manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree to its submission to *Drones*.
