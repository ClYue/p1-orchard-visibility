# Figure captions

Captions below match the figure files in this package and the manuscript.

**Figure 1.** Visibility of the marker pair in the alley centre (a,b) and near the canopy edge (c,d), before applying the camera gate. The left panels show the ray-based lookup tables. The right panels show the corresponding bounded, centred Gaussian fits. Offsets are measured from the nominal centre of the marker pair. The cross marks the zero-offset point, and the dashed contours mark pair visibility of 0.5. Pair visibility at zero offset and the coefficient of determination of the Gaussian fit are reported in Section 2.3.

**Figure 2.** Localisation error and UAV flight distance across 30 simulated trials per configuration. Panels (a,b) show the alley centre and panels (c,d) the canopy edge. Grey points represent 48 fixed stations per scene. The star identifies the fixed station with the lowest mean heading RMSE. The coloured markers show the four motion weights tested for each adaptive prior, and the open ring identifies the lookup-table setting at $w=0.001$. The vertical axes are logarithmic. Flight distance is the observed cumulative distance for each configuration.

**Figure 3.** Effect of requested station azimuth on heading RMSE (a) and the paired-detection fraction (b) at a requested radius of 2 m. Azimuth is measured in the vehicle frame, with 0° forward and positive angles counterclockwise. Points show the means of 30 trials. Error bars show pointwise 95% percentile bootstrap intervals based on 5,000 resamples of the trial seeds. The shaded band marks the sampled azimuths 45°, 90°, and 135°, for which paired detections were uncommon on the canopy-edge path. Curves are shape-preserving interpolations through the eight tested azimuths, added to aid reading; they are not fitted models.

**Figure 4.** Effect of lateral path offset along the canopy-edge path. (a) Heading RMSE of the best of the 48 fixed stations at each offset. The shaded band marks where the error stays within 0.002° of its floor; the dashed lines mark the alley edges at ±0.8 m, beyond which the path runs inside a crown. (b) Azimuth of the best station, with 0° forward. The switch is asymmetric: the azimuth changes between 0.6 and 0.8 m on the positive side but only between −0.8 and −1.0 m on the negative side, because the planting jitter is not mirrored.

**Figure 5.** Heading RMSE (a) and cumulative UAV flight distance (b) across four crown widths in the canopy-edge scenario. Each point is a mean over 30 trials. For each width, the fixed comparator is the station with the lowest mean heading RMSE among the 48 tested stations. The lookup-table policy uses the same motion weight, $w=0.001$, at every width. Curves are shape-preserving interpolations through the four tested widths, added to aid reading; they are not fitted models. Flight distance is an outcome of each policy. Error bars show pointwise 95% percentile bootstrap intervals for the selected configurations, based on 5,000 resamples of the 30 seeds; the intervals are conditional on the selection.

**Figure 6.** Independent Gazebo rendering check of the visibility model. (a) Predicted transmittance against rendered visible fraction for the 32 stations inside the camera gate, coloured by station radius; the dashed line marks equality. (b) Fraction of stations with both markers visible as a function of radius for the model (transmittance ≥ 0.5) and for the Gazebo rendering; the two series are offset horizontally for readability and coincide at every radius. Pearson's r in (a) is 0.987 and Spearman's ρ is 0.936.
