# P1 配图替换说明

四张主图保留现有编号。将对应 PNG 放入 Word，建议显示宽度约 156 mm。PDF 可用于矢量核对和后续排版。方法示意图为可选补充图，若使用请在方法节引用 Figure S1。新版图注作为替换文本提供。

**Figure 1.** Marker-pair visibility in the alley center (a,b) and at the canopy edge (c,d) before applying the camera gate. The left column shows the ray-based lookup tables, and the right column shows the bounded Gaussian fits with the center fixed at zero offset. The cross marks zero offset and the dashed contour marks pair visibility of 0.5. The horizontal display range is −15 to 15 m along the row and −12 to 12 m across the row. The labels report visibility at zero offset ($p_0$) or goodness of fit ($R^2$).

**Figure 2.** Mean heading RMSE (a,c) and position RMSE (b,d) against cumulative UAV flight distance across 30 trials per configuration. Panels (a,b) show the alley center and panels (c,d) show the canopy edge. Gray circles represent 48 fixed stations per scene. The star marks the fixed station with the lowest mean heading RMSE. The colored markers show the tested motion weights for each adaptive prior, and the open ring marks the lookup table at $w=0.001$. Panel (c) labels the two operating points compared in the abstract. The error axes use logarithmic scales.

**Figure 3.** Mean heading RMSE (a) and marker-pair detection rate (b) across eight requested azimuths at a 2 m requested station radius. Azimuth is measured counterclockwise from the forward direction in the vehicle frame. Error bars show pointwise 95% percentile bootstrap intervals from 5,000 resamples of the 30 trial seeds. Shading identifies the sampled near-side azimuths at 45°, 90°, and 135°.

**Figure 4.** Mean heading RMSE (a) and cumulative UAV flight distance (b) at four across-row crown semi-axes on the canopy-edge path. The fixed comparator is the station with the lowest mean heading RMSE among the tested fixed stations at each width. The adaptive lookup-table policy uses $w=0.001$ throughout. Error bars show pointwise 95% percentile bootstrap intervals for the selected configurations, based on 5,000 resamples of 30 seeds. Lines connect the sampled crown widths.

**Optional Figure S1.** Schematic of the simulated orchard and camera observations. (a) Plan view of the nominal center and offset paths, orchard row spacing and marker baseline. (b) Cross-row projection of illustrative rays from the UAV to one of the two markers at the offset path. The near-side ray passes through the canopy.
