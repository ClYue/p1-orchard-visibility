# Figure captions for the revised figures

**Figure 1.** Visibility of the marker pair in the alley center (a,b) and near the canopy edge (c,d), before applying the camera gate. The left panels show the ray-based lookup tables. The right panels show the corresponding bounded, centered Gaussian fits. Offsets are measured from the nominal center of the marker pair. The cross marks zero horizontal offset, and the dashed contour marks pair visibility of 0.5. The labels report the lookup-table value at zero offset ($p_0$) and goodness of fit ($R^2$).

**Figure 2.** Localization error and UAV flight distance across 30 simulated trials per configuration. Panels (a,b) show the alley center and panels (c,d) the canopy edge. Gray points represent 48 fixed stations per scene. The star identifies the fixed station with the lowest mean heading RMSE. The three colored series show the four motion weights tested for each adaptive prior, and the open ring identifies the lookup-table setting at $w=0.001$. The vertical axes are logarithmic. Flight distance is the observed cumulative distance for each configuration.

**Figure 3.** Effect of requested station azimuth on heading RMSE (a) and paired marker detections (b) at a requested radius of 2 m. Azimuth is measured in the vehicle frame, with 0° forward and positive angles counterclockwise. Points show the means of 30 trials. Error bars show pointwise 95% percentile bootstrap intervals based on 5,000 resamples of the trial seeds. The shaded band marks the sampled azimuths 45°, 90°, and 135°, for which paired detections were uncommon on the canopy-edge path.

**Figure 4.** Heading RMSE (a) and cumulative UAV flight distance (b) across four crown widths in the canopy-edge scenario. Each point is a mean over 30 trials. For each width, the fixed comparator is the station with the lowest mean heading RMSE among the 48 tested stations. The lookup-table policy uses the same motion weight, $w=0.001$, at every width. The lines connect tested widths, and flight distance is an outcome of each policy.
