| 引言第 1 段、文献 [1] | "degradation in proportion to crown density and observation epoch" 方向写反且过强 | Feng 等（Remote Sensing 2021, 13, 2325）用多历元观测**降低**误差，且回归拟合不支持"成比例"；该文明示 canopy density 与精度相关，single canopy size 无明显影响。改为 "with canopy density closely related to positioning accuracy [1,2,3], while multi-epoch observations reduce but do not remove the error [1]" |

| 引言第 2 段、文献 [24] | 把 UWB 测距写成 mutual bearing | De Silva 与 Katupitiya（Sensors 2024, 24, 4629）原文为 "UWB range-only localisation"，Decawave TREK-1000。改为 "with UAV-to-UGV ranging [24]"，bearing 观测改引 [10] |
