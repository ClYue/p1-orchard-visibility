# Cover letter

Dear Editors,

We submit “Canopy Visibility Constrains UAV Viewpoint Selection: A Simulation Study in Orchards” for consideration as an Article in *Drones*.

We examine where a UAV should observe a two-marker ground robot when the orchard canopy can block its view. We compared 48 fixed stations with adaptive policies that use a lookup table, a bounded Gaussian, or a uniform visibility prior. All policies were evaluated using the same estimator and detections drawn from ray intersections at the true marker positions.

The centerline and canopy-edge scenarios gave different results. At a 2 m requested station radius, heading RMSE ranged from 0.359° to 0.737° across azimuths on the centerline and from 0.562° to 8.792° at the edge. Compared with the fixed station that achieved the lowest heading RMSE at the edge, the selected lookup-table configuration reduced mean flight distance from 31.2 m to 26.4 m (15.6%) while increasing heading RMSE from 0.562° to 0.749°. A four-width sensitivity analysis reports the same comparison using a common adaptive motion weight. The study connects aerial viewpoint selection to ground localization under simulated canopy occlusion. The supplement includes the simulator, figure scripts, and seed-level results.

We confirm that the manuscript has not been published previously and is not under consideration elsewhere. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree to its submission to *Drones*. Chunlong Yue and Junyi Zhang are the corresponding authors. The authors declare no conflicts of interest.
